# Legend Cards & Archetype Updates - v2.1

## ⭐ Legend Cards System

### What Are Legend Cards?

**Legend cards** are special, rare cards representing the most significant figures in Buddhist history. They feature:

✨ **Golden border** (thicker than regular cards)  
⭐ **Star icon** in the top right corner  
🌟 **Extra special** in gameplay and lore  

---

## The 4 Legend Cards

### 1. ⭐ **Buddha (The Enlightened Sage)**
**Archetype**: The Awakened One

**Why Legend**: 
- The founder of Buddhism
- Achieved perfect enlightenment
- Only being with 100 in ALL stats
- Most powerful card in the game

**Stats**: All 100s (Power, Wisdom, Resolve, Influence, Transformation, Merit)

---

### 2. ⭐ **Siddhartha Gautama (The Prince)**
**Archetype**: The Bodhisatta

**Why Legend**:
- Buddha before enlightenment
- The Bodhisatta (being destined for Buddhahood)
- Represents the journey to awakening
- Historical turning point

**Special**: Shows Buddha in his pre-enlightenment form as Prince Siddhartha

---

### 3. ⭐ **Angulimala**
**Archetype**: Redeemed Disciple

**Why Legend**:
- Most dramatic transformation in Buddhism
- Serial killer → Arahant
- Ultimate redemption story
- Has 100 Transformation stat (only card besides Buddha)

**Famous Story**: Converted by Buddha, became fully enlightened despite killing 999 people

---

### 4. ⭐ **Vessantara**
**Archetype**: Jātaka Tale

**Why Legend**:
- Buddha's last life before final birth
- Perfected the virtue of generosity (dāna)
- Bridge between past lives and Buddhahood
- Represents the culmination of many lifetimes

**Special**: Only Jātaka tale (past life story) card in the game

## Gameplay Impact

### Legend Cards Are:
✅ **Powerful** - Top-tier stats  
✅ **Rare** - Only 4 in deck (11%)  
✅ **Collectible** - Special feeling to win them  
✅ **Educational** - Highlight most important figures  

### Strategy
- **Buddha** = Ultimate card (all 100s)
- **Angulimala** = 100 Transformation (highest single stat besides Buddha)
- **Vessantara** = 88 Merit, good all-around
- **Bodhisatta** = 75 Merit (pre-enlightenment)

**From Archetypes**:

**From Legends**:
- Who was most important in Buddhist history
- The Buddha's journey (Bodhisatta → Buddha)
- Greatest transformation story (Angulimala)
- Past life connection (Vessantara)

**From Foremost Disciples**:
- Etadagga Sutta classifications
- Different types of excellence
- First sangha vs later disciples
- Añña Kondañña's special place as first arahant
