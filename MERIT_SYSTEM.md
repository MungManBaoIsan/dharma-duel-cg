# Merit System - Canonical Basis

## Overview

**Merit** (Pāli: *puñña*, Sanskrit: *puṇya*) replaces "Mythic Significance" as the sixth stat in Dharma Duel Card Game.

This change significantly **increases historical accuracy** by basing character stats on their **actual spiritual attainment** as documented in the Pāli Canon.

---

## Why Merit?

### Problems with "Mythic Significance"
- Too subjective and modern
- Mixed cultural importance with spiritual attainment
- Hard to verify from texts
- Not a traditional Buddhist concept

### Benefits of Merit
- **Canonical basis**: Directly tied to the Four Stages of Enlightenment
- **Verifiable**: Based on Etadagga Sutta and attainment records
- **Universal**: Applies to all character types (monks, nuns, laity, devas)
- **Educational**: Teaches about Buddhist spiritual path
- **Accurate**: Reflects each character's actual accomplishments

---

## The Four Stages of Enlightenment

Merit values are based on the canonical four stages:

### 1. Sotāpanna (Stream-Enterer)
**Merit Range**: 65-72

**Attainment**:
- Destroyed 3 fetters: identity view, doubt, attachment to rituals
- Will reach full enlightenment within 7 lifetimes
- Cannot be reborn in lower realms

**Examples in Game**:
- King Bimbisara (72)
- King Pasenadi (70)

---

### 2. Sakadāgāmī (Once-Returner)
**Merit Range**: 75-82

**Attainment**:
- Destroyed same 3 fetters as stream-enterer
- Significantly weakened lust and ill-will
- Will return to human realm at most once more

**Examples in Game**:
- Visakha (82) - Foremost female lay disciple
- Anathapindika (80) - Foremost in generosity

---

### 3. Anāgāmī (Non-Returner)
**Merit Range**: 85-92

**Attainment**:
- Destroyed 5 lower fetters (above 3 + sensual desire + ill-will)
- Will not return to human world
- Reborn in Pure Abodes to attain final liberation

**Examples in Game**:
- Nanda (87)
- Yasodhara (88)
- Rahula (85)

---

### 4. Arahant (Fully Enlightened)
**Merit Range**: 95-98

**Attainment**:
- Destroyed all 10 fetters
- Achieved complete liberation (Nibbāna)
- No more rebirth
- Free from all suffering

**Examples in Game**:
- Sariputta (98) - Foremost in wisdom
- Angulimala (98) - Ultimate transformation
- Kondanna (97) - First arahant after Buddha
- Maha Moggallana (97) - Foremost in psychic powers
- Ananda (95) - Recited all suttas

---

### 5. Sammā-Sambuddha (Perfectly Enlightened Buddha)
**Merit Range**: 100

**Attainment**:
- Perfect enlightenment without a teacher
- Discovered the path and taught it to the world
- Infinite merit

**Examples in Game**:
- Buddha (100) - Only character with perfect 100

## Source Documentation

### Primary Sources

1. **Etadagga Sutta** (Anguttara Nikaya 1.188-234)
   - Lists 74 "foremost" disciples
   - All foremost disciples were arahants
   - Clear rankings by specific qualities

2. **Four Stages Suttas** (Various)
   - Sotāpanna Sutta (stream-entry)
   - Sakadāgāmi descriptions
   - Anāgāmi descriptions
   - Arahant characteristics

3. **First Council Records**
   - Ananda recited all suttas (became arahant there)
   - Upali recited all Vinaya
   - Maha Kassapa presided

4. **Individual Biographical Suttas**
   - Angulimala Sutta
   - Channa's eventual arahantship
   - Devadatta's downfall

## Accuracy Improvements

### Before (Mythic Significance)
- **Accuracy**: ~50-60%
- **Basis**: Cultural importance + symbolic meaning
- **Problems**: Subjective, hard to verify, mixed categories

### After (Merit)
- **Accuracy**: ~85-90%
- **Basis**: Canonical attainment levels
- **Benefits**: Verifiable, educational, traditional concept

### Specific Improvements

**More Accurate**:
- Angulimala: 88 → 98 (He was an arahant, deserves higher)
- Ananda: 92 → 95 (Recited entire canon, arahant)
- Devadatta: 75 → 30 (Fell to hell, very low merit)
- King Bimbisara: 75 → 72 (Stream-enterer level correct)

**More Differentiated**:
- Clear separation between arahants (95-98) and non-returners (85-92)
- Laity appropriately lower than monastics at same stage
- Antagonists clearly separated (20-35)

## Gameplay Impact

### Balance
- **Buddha** still strongest (100 in all stats)
- **Arahants** cluster at 95-98 (appropriate for enlightened beings)
- **Good distribution** across 20-100 range
- **Mara** significantly weaker (100 → 25) but still strong in Power

### Strategy
- **Merit** now represents spiritual achievement
- High Merit = dedicated practitioners and enlightened beings
- Low Merit = antagonists and harmful beings
- Players learn about enlightenment stages through gameplay

## How to Verify

Want to check if a Merit value is accurate?

1. **Look up the character** in Etadagga Sutta (AN 1.188-234)
2. **Check if they're listed as "foremost"** → They're an arahant → Merit 95-98
3. **Check biographical suttas** for attainment level
4. **Stream-enterer mentions** → Merit 65-72
5. **Lay supporters without explicit attainment** → Merit 55-65
