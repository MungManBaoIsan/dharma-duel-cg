# 🎴 Dharma Duel: Legends of Ancient India

**An epic card battle featuring 40 legendary figures from Buddhist history**

Journey to ancient India, 2,500 years ago, where wisdom and enlightenment hang in the balance. As the Buddha's teachings spread across the land, powerful forces align—some to illuminate the path, others to obscure it. Now, in a clash of legends, you must master the strengths and weaknesses of history's greatest spiritual warriors, wise teachers, and mythical beings.

---

## 📖 The Legend

*In the sacred groves of ancient India, a mystical tournament unfolds...*

The **Dharma Council** has convened—an assembly of the greatest minds and spirits from Buddha's era. From the enlightened Buddha himself to fierce Emperor Ashoka, from the wise Sariputta to the tempting Mara, forty legendary beings have gathered to test their mastery of Power, Wisdom, Resolve, Influence, Transformation, and Merit.

But this is no ordinary contest. Each battle reveals profound truths about the nature of enlightenment. The reformed courtesan Amrapali stands equal to mighty emperors. The gentle benefactor Sujata's merit rivals that of powerful deities. Even Mara, lord of delusion, commands respect for his influence over mortal minds.

**You are summoned** to lead these legends in strategic duels. Study their strengths, exploit their weaknesses, and discover which virtues triumph when enlightenment itself is at stake. Will Wisdom overcome Power? Can Transformation defeat Influence? The fate of the Dharma depends on your insight.

**The council awaits. Choose your champion. Let the Dharma Duel begin!**

## 🎯 How to Win

### The Basics
1. **40 cards split** - You get 20, opponent gets 20
2. **Choose wisely** - Pick a stat you think will win
3. **Higher wins** - Winner takes both cards to their deck
4. **Ties = Battle** - Cards go to battle pile, next winner takes all!
5. **Collect all 40** - First to claim every card wins!

### Master Strategy
🧠 **Know Your Cards**
- Buddha has 4 perfect stats (100) but weak Power (95)
- Trap cards like Sujata (Power 35) punish careless choices
- Ashoka's Influence (95) beats even Buddha (90)!

🎭 **Exploit Weaknesses**
- High Power cards often have low Wisdom
- Mara forces have weak Merit and Transformation
- Spiritual masters may lack worldly Influence

🔥 **Advanced Tactics**
- Remember which cards opponent played
- Track battle pile size (more cards at stake!)
- Adapt strategy based on deck composition
