## ✅ Battle System - FULLY WORKING!

### How Tie Battles Work

When you and your opponent tie on a stat, the **battle system** activates!

```
Round 1: You choose Power
Your card: Power 85
Opponent: Power 85
→ TIE!

What happens:
1. Both cards go to BATTLE PILE (face-up)
2. Battle pile now has 2 cards
3. Both players draw next card
4. SAME PLAYER chooses stat again
5. Winner takes ALL cards (2 new + 2 from pile = 4 total!)
```
