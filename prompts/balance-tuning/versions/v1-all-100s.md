# v1 — Buddha at All 100s (replaced)

Initial stats: Power 100, Wisdom 100, Resolve 100, Influence 100, Transformation 100, Merit 100.

## Problems

1. **Zero strategy required.** Any stat chosen = guaranteed win. The card was a dead end for strategic thinking.
2. **Theological inaccuracy.** Power 100 implies supreme martial/physical dominance. Influence 100 implies supreme political power. Neither fits a figure who renounced both.
3. **Frustrating when opponent draws it.** Because you couldn't win regardless of your card, the game felt determined by luck of draw alone.

## Why the change happened

Question asked: "How to make Buddha beatable?" — prompted a review of 5 balance options before settling on contextual weaknesses (Option 1): lower the stats that don't fit the theological profile, keep the ones that do.
