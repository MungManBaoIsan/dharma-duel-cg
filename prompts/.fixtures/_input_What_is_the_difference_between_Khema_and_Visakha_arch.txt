Khema archetype: Chief Bhikkhunis
Visakha archetype: Chief Laywomen

These are different archetypes. The original label "Famous Female Disciples" was replaced because it conflated ordained nuns with lay women.

Khema and Uppalavanna are Chief Bhikkhunis - ordained nuns (bhikkhunis) living under the Vinaya rules.
Visakha and Queen Mallika are Chief Laywomen - lay female supporters who remained householders.

Other replaced archetypes: "Other Great Disciples" became "Foremost Disciples". "Family & Early Life" became "The Sakya Clan". Angulimala became "Redeemed Disciple" instead of generic. Legend cards: Buddha, Siddhartha, Angulimala, Vessantara.

The legend system marks 4 special cards with gold borders. No deprecated archetypes remain in the current cards.json.
