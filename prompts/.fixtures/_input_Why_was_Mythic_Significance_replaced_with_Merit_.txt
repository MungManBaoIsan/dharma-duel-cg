Mythic Significance was replaced with Merit. Mythic Significance had no canonical source in the Pali Canon.

Merit (punna) is grounded in the Four Stages of Enlightenment and the Etadagga Sutta (AN 1.188-234).

Merit is universal - it applies to all 40 figures: monks, kings, deities, Mara, and Devadatta all have merit scores. Mara has merit 25, Devadatta has merit 30 - both correctly low for antagonist figures.

Canonical scale used:
- 100 = Samma-Sambuddha (Buddha alone)
- 95-98 = Arahant (fully enlightened)
- 65-72 = Stream-enterer (Sotapanna)

Historical accuracy for the sixth stat rose from approximately 50-60% to 85-90% after this change. The old field name no longer appears anywhere in the updated database.
