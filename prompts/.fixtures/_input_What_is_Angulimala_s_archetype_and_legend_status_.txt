Angulimala archetype: Redeemed Disciple
legend: true

Angulimala is one of 4 legend cards (alongside Buddha, Siddhartha, Vessantara).

The archetype was chosen specifically to highlight his unique transformation narrative rather than using a generic category label.

The split between Chief Bhikkhunis (ordained nuns like Khema) and Chief Laywomen (lay supporters like Visakha) reflects important Buddhist distinctions.

Legend cards have gold borders and a star icon. Only 4 of 40 cards have legend status, making them rare and exciting to draw.
