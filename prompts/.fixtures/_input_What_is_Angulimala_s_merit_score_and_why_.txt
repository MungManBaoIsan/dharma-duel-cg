Angulimala merit score: 98

This is arahant level on the canonical Four Stages of Enlightenment scale.
The Etadagga Sutta (AN 1.188-234) from the Pali Canon provides the basis for this assignment.

Merit scale:
- 100 = Buddha (Samma-Sambuddha)
- 95-98 = Arahant (fully enlightened)
- 85-92 = Non-returner
- 75-82 = Once-returner

Angulimala achieved complete arahantship despite his violent past. Mara, Devadatta and the antagonist figures have much lower merit scores (below 35).

Merit is now used instead of Mythic Significance because it is grounded in canonical Pali sources.
