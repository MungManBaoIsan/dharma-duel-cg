# Reasoning: Archetype Naming System

## Goal

Replace generic AI-generated archetype labels with names that were both theologically accurate and clear to non-expert players. The archetype appears prominently on every card as the first descriptor below the name — it had to earn its place. The original labels ("Other Great Disciples", "Mythic, Cosmic & Supernatural", "Famous Female Disciples") were imprecise and forgettable.

## Iteration history

**v1 — AI-generated labels:** Groupings like "Other Great Disciples" (too vague), "The Two Chief Disciples" (redundant with card count), and "Famous Female Disciples" (conflates ordained nuns with lay women). These were generated automatically and reflected no particular Buddhist knowledge.

**v2 — User-selected labels (current):** The user went through 15+ categories one by one, judging each on two criteria simultaneously: accuracy (does this match Buddhist terminology and important distinctions?) and clarity (can a non-expert understand it?).

Key design decisions:

- **"Famous Female Disciples" split into two:** "Chief Bhikkhunis" (Khema, Uppalavanna — ordained nuns) vs "Chief Laywomen" (Visakha, Queen Mallika — lay supporters). Mixing the two erases a meaningful Buddhist distinction.
- **Siddhartha → "The Bodhisatta":** Precise Pali term for a being destined for Buddhahood but not yet enlightened. Captures the card's specific narrative position.
- **Angulimala → "Redeemed Disciple":** Highlights the transformation arc that makes this card one of the most powerful in the deck.
- **Devadatta → "The Adversary":** Captures his role without the clinical term "schismatic" or the vague "cousin."
- **Individual Five Ascetics removed, Añña Kondañña added:** The group card ("First Sangha") covers the five together; Kondañña deserves his own card as the first person to understand the Dharma after the Buddha's first teaching.

## Failure modes the final version handles

- **Conflated categories** — "Famous Female Disciples" was ambiguous about lay vs ordained status. The split prevents this.
- **Redundant information** — "The Two Chief Disciples" tells players the count, not the role. "Chief Disciples" is cleaner.
- **Missed narrative significance** — "Other Great Disciples" hides that these figures are from a canonical list (Etadagga Sutta). "Foremost Disciples" signals their textual status.

## Outcome

16 distinct archetypes covering all 40 cards. Four Legend cards established: Buddha, Siddhartha (The Bodhisatta), Angulimala, and Vessantara. These four were chosen because they represent the most significant transformation arcs in the Buddhist tradition, and keeping Legend cards rare (4 of 40 = 10%) ensures that drawing one feels genuinely special rather than routine.

## What I'd change next

Nothing — the archetype system is one of the most carefully considered parts of the project.
