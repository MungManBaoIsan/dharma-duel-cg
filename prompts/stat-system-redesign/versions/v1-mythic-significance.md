# v1 — Mythic Significance (deprecated)

The original sixth stat was called "Mythic Significance" and was assigned based on how cosmologically prominent or symbolically important a figure was in Buddhist tradition and iconography.

## Why it was discarded

- **Not grounded in any canonical source.** Two equally knowledgeable people could assign different scores to the same figure.
- **Not a traditional Buddhist concept.** There is no Pali or Sanskrit equivalent to "Mythic Significance."
- **Inconsistent across character types.** It worked for deities and cosmic beings, but was harder to assign meaningfully to ordinary lay supporters.
- **Estimated historical accuracy: ~50–60%.**

## Example assignments (v1)

| Card | Mythic Significance | Reason given |
|------|--------------------|----|
| Mara | 100 | Primary antagonist, cosmic-level significance |
| Mucalinda | 88 | Famous iconic image of naga protecting Buddha |
| Kondanna | 90 | First arahant after Buddha |
| Anathapindika | 72 | Important but not cosmologically prominent |
