GAME CONTROLS:
• Mouse - Click stats on your turn
• Mouse - Click anywhere to skip result screen
• Close Window - Quit game

COLOR SCHEME:
• Background: Deep maroon (traditional Buddhist color)
• Highlights: Gold and saffron orange
• Cards: Cream/beige with brown borders
• Text: Black and dark gray
• Results: Green (win), Red (lose), Blue (tie)
