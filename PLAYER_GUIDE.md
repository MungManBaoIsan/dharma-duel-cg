# 🎮 Dharma Duel - Quick Start Guide for Players

## How to Play RIGHT NOW (30 seconds)

### Step 1: Install Python & Pygame
```bash
# Download Python from: https://www.python.org/downloads/
# Then run:
pip install pygame
```

### Step 2: Run the Game
```bash
python main.py
```

### Step 3: Start Playing!
- Click "VS COMPUTER" → Select difficulty → Play!
- Click "VS PLAYER" → Pass & Play with a friend!

---

## 🎯 Basic Rules (2 minutes to learn)

1. **Start** - Each player gets 20 random cards
2. **Your Turn** - Click a stat on YOUR card (the one you can see)
3. **Compare** - Both cards flip over, highest stat wins
4. **Winner** - Takes both cards and controls next round
5. **Win** - First to collect all 40 cards wins!

### Special: Battle Mode (Ties)
- Same stat value? Both cards go to battle pile
- Next round winner takes all battle pile cards!
- Can build up to 10+ cards at stake!

---

## 🕹️ Game Controls

### VS Computer Mode
1. Main Menu → "VS COMPUTER"
2. Select difficulty:
   - **Easy** - Great for learning (AI makes lots of mistakes)
   - **Moderate** - Balanced challenge
   - **Hard** - Tough opponent (AI plays smart)
   - **Smart** - Most fun! (Behaves like a strategic human)
3. On your turn: Click a stat on your card (left side)
4. Click [NEXT ROUND →] to see next card
5. Repeat until someone has all 40 cards!

### VS Player Mode (Pass & Play)
1. Main Menu → "VS PLAYER"
2. Player 1 is LEFT, Player 2 is RIGHT
3. Active player's card is visible, opponent's is hidden
4. Active player clicks a stat on THEIR OWN card
5. Both cards flip, winner gains cards
6. Winner controls next round
7. Pass the device between players!

## 💡 Quick Strategy Tips

### For Beginners
- Buddha is strongest (but not unbeatable!)
- Look for your card's HIGHEST stats
- Watch out for trap stats (below 40)

### Getting Better
- Remember which cards opponent played
- Track battle pile size (more cards = more risk/reward)
- Some cards have multiple high stats, use them wisely

### Advanced Play
- Spiritual masters (monks/nuns) → High Wisdom/Merit, Low Power
- Warriors/rulers → High Power/Influence, Lower spiritual stats
- Mara forces → Strong Power but weak Merit/Transformation
- Trap cards like Sujata (Power 35) can steal wins!

## 🎨 What Do the Colors Mean?

### Card Colors (Win/Loss)
- 🟢 **GREEN** - You won this round!
- 🔴 **RED** - You lost this round
- ⚪ **WHITE** - Not yet compared

### Card Borders (Tier System)
- 🏆 **Gold border** - Platinum/Gold tier (strongest)
- 🥈 **Silver border** - Silver tier (mid-level)
- 🥉 **Bronze border** - Bronze tier (weakest)

## 📊 Understanding Stats

Each card has 6 stats (range: 25-100):

| Stat | What It Means | Example High | Example Low |
|------|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| ⚔️ **Power** | Physical/Psychic strength | Mara (95), Ashoka (92) | Sujata (35), Khema (55) |
| 🧠 **Wisdom** | Understanding/Insight | Buddha (100), Sariputta (98) | Mara's Army (40) |
| 💎 **Resolve** | Willpower/Determination | Buddha (100), Angulimala (95) | Visakha (80) |
| 👑 **Influence** | Political/Social impact | Ashoka (95), Bimbisara (90) | Sujata (50) |
| 🦋 **Transform** | Capacity for change | Angulimala (100), Buddha (100) | Mara (25-30) |
| ✨ **Merit** | Spiritual virtue | Buddha (100), Many saints (95+) | Mara (25-30) |

**Pro tip:** Buddha has 4 perfect stats (100) but only 95 Power - use this wisely!

## 🆘 Common Questions

**Q: My card has all low stats, what do I do?**
A: Pick your least-bad stat! Sometimes you'll get weak cards, that's part of the game.

**Q: What happens on a tie?**
A: Battle mode! Both cards go to a pile, winner of next round gets all.

**Q: Can I see opponent's card before choosing?**
A: No - that's the strategy! You only see your own card until after you choose.

**Q: How long does a game take?**
A: Usually 5-10 minutes. Quick rounds, strategic depth!

**Q: Is Buddha unbeatable?**
A: Almost! Buddha has 4 perfect stats (100) but only 95 Power. Cards like Mara (95 Power) can beat Buddha!

**Q: What's the best card?**
A: Buddha (rating 97.5) but many Gold tier cards can win specific matchups!
